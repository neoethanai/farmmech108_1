Government Support

Schemes We Specialize In

We help farmers and agri-businesses access the full range of central and state government benefits available to the agricultural sector.

PM
PM-KISAN
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi - direct income support of Rs. 6000/year to eligible farmers. Application, verification, and correction support.
SM
SMAM - Sub Mission on Agri Mechanization
Subsidy assistance for farm machinery purchases. Help with applications, dealer coordination, and disbursement follow-up.
RK
RKVY - Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana
Project preparation and funding applications for agricultural infrastructure, storage, processing, and marketing under RKVY.
GE
GeM Portal - Government e-Marketplace
End-to-end GeM registration, catalog management, bid participation, and order fulfillment support for agri-product sellers.
MS
MSME & Udyam Registration
Official MSME registration enabling access to priority sector lending, government tender preferences, and business subsidies.
PM
PMFBY - Crop Insurance
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana enrollment assistance, claim filing support, and settlement coordination for distressed farmers.
